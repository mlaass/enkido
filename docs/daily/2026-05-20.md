- **fix(cedar): preserve SEQPAT_QUERY cycle cache across hot-swap** (`694652c6`)  
  `init_sequence_program` was allocating a fresh output buffer on every recompile (setting `num_events = 0`), causing the cycle-cache hit check to fail and firing an extra `step++` per recompile on ALTERNATE sub-sequences — the "playback index gets reset" regression.   Seven opcodes migrated: `comp` (attack/release), `limiter` (lookahead), `gate` (attack/hold/release + dry/wet), `dattorro` (damping/mod_depth/lfo_rate + dry/wet), `flanger` (feedback + lfo_phase/dry/wet), `comb` (damping), `seqpat_transport` (cycle_length off the inputs[3]/[4] halving).
