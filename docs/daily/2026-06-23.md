## cedar/ — 4 commits, 13 files touched

- **Phase 1 — null-buffer guard + arena reclaim primitive** (`8ea137d`)  
  Stops the SIGSEGV (null comb delay-line in `reverbs.hpp`) by adding `buffers_ready()` checks to every arena-backed FX opcode; on exhaustion the opcode degrades to dry passthrough and logs once via the new `CEDAR_LOG_ONCE` latch (`cedar/util/log_once.hpp`). Adds the reclamation primitive to `AudioArena`: a size-classed, allocation-free free list (power-of-two float classes, intrusive next-pointer in the block's own memory) with `allocate()` reuse, `release()`, and `reset()`. Each arena-owning DSP state gets `buffers_ready()` + `release(AudioArena&)`; `DelayState`/`PingPongDelayState` also reclaim the old block on `grow()`. New `test_arena_reclaim.cpp` covers free-list reuse, re-zero, cross-class, grow, overflow, and state release. Opcodes updated: delays, reverbs, modulation, sequence.

- **Phase 2 — activate arena reclamation in GC** (`c20eaf2`)  
  Wires the Phase 1 free list into the state lifecycle. `StatePool` gains `set_arena()` and an internal `release_state_buffers()` that `std::visit`s the evicted `DSPState` variant (`HasArenaRelease` concept makes stateless members no-ops). `gc_sweep()` releases buffers only on the immediate-delete branch; states in the fading pool keep buffers until `gc_fading()` removes them (prevents freed-buffer reuse under a still-crossfading program). `VM::VM()` wires `state_pool_` to `audio_arena_` (shadow pool intentionally left unwired). Adds `VM::arena_bytes_used()`.

- **Phase 3 — drift fuzz becomes the regression guard** (`19e7bb6`)  
  Drops the VM-recreation workaround: the drift fuzz now hot-swaps every mutated program into a single persistent VM for the full run. Verified to 10 000 iterations (arena peak ~12 MB, zero exhaustions). Adds `AudioArena::exhaustion_count` (surfaced as `VM::arena_exhaustion_count`); the guard asserts `exhaustions == 0` (scale-insensitive) plus a secondary `< 3/4` high-water bound.

- **PRD marked complete** (`4e588a5`)  
  Updates `docs/prd-audio-arena-reclamation.md` status to DONE: all three phases shipped and verified (cedar + akkado suites green, drift fuzz clean to 10k iters, ASan/UBSan/LSan-clean).
